About Me

Collector first, seller second — building a personal collection around cards that are fun to own and helping other collectors find cards they’ll enjoy just as much.

What I Collect

My personal collection focuses heavily on Green Bay Packers rookies, rookie patches and rookie autographs, with an emphasis on eye appeal rather than simply chasing the most expensive card. Sterling Sharpe and Robin Yount are two of my favorite players, and I also collect select Brewers and soccer rookies.

Collector & Seller

I sell sports cards across Whatnot, eBay and CollX under the PackFanNFL/Tundra Sports Cards banner. You’ll find raw singles, graded cards, rookies, parallels, autographs, numbered cards and other interesting pieces, with weekly Whatnot auctions alongside marketplace listings.

Why I Sell

This is a hobby-driven shop. Selling helps support additions to my own personal collection, while giving other collectors a chance to find cards for theirs. One of the things I enjoy most is getting Packers cards into the hands of collectors who will appreciate and keep them in their PCs.
